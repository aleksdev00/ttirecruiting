# Copy deck

## Core copy

- Hero: `Find a CDL-A job that fits your experience and home time.`
- Supporting line: `TTI Recruiting helps you compare approved opportunities and reach the right next step. The hiring carrier makes the final employment decision.`
- Primary CTA: `Find My Next Driving Job`
- Secondary CTA: `Talk With a Recruiter`
- W-2 label: `W-2 application`
- 1099 label: `Recruiter-assisted 1099 intake`
- Contact form intro: `Tell us how to reach you and what kind of driving work you are considering. This is a conversation request, not a completed job application.`

## Honest state copy

- Empty catalog: `We do not have a confirmed match for those filters right now. A recruiter can still talk through what may fit.`
- Expired offer: `This opportunity is no longer accepting public interest. Browse current opportunities or ask a recruiter about alternatives.`
- Unavailable integration: `Your information was not sent because this connection is not configured. Please use the approved recruiter contact option instead.`
- Validation error: `Check the highlighted fields and try again.`
- Pending save: `Saving your request...`
- Confirmed contact request: `Your request is saved. A recruiter will review it through the approved process. We do not promise a response time here.`
- Failed save: `We could not save your request. Nothing was submitted. Try again or use the listed contact option.`
- W-2 handoff: `You are leaving TTI Recruiting to continue in the existing driver application. TTI has not received a completed application from this click.`
- 1099 next step: `A recruiter must guide this intake. Do not send CDL or medical documents through this public form.`

## CTA rules

Every application CTA must name its actual destination. Never use `Apply now` for a contact request, and never show a success receipt before durable acceptance.
